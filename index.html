Our Story

Garcia’s is a family-run sandwich shop serving comfort food with bold flavours and a passion for Italian cooking. We keep our food yummy and our lines moving.

Our sandwiches start with tomato sauce made fresh in house every day and Ontario-raised veal, pork, beef and chicken: crispy cutlets, house-made sauces and bakery-fresh buns, with fresh-cut ingredients every day.

Round it out with a creamy espresso, crème espresso or frothy cappuccino, and a treat from our display counter of fresh gelato and desserts.

Derryk & Shantel Garcia, owners
